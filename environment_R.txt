"Times New Roman" must be available to the R graphics devices; the shared
style aliases the base-14 Times faces when the named family is absent
(scripts_r/pub_style_r.R). The strict panel-alignment audit is optional: it
runs only when PANEL_AUDIT_SCRIPTS points at the auditing toolchain and
degrades to writing the layout manifest otherwise.
